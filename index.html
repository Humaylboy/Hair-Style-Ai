Guide

How to use HairGuru

From a selfie to your new look in nine steps.

  1. 1

    Read the photo guide

    Tap 📋 Photo guide (it also pops up the first time). Face the camera straight, keep both shoulders in the frame, leave space above your head, pull hair off your forehead and ears, and stand in even light.

    A good photo means accurate results
  2. 2

    Take or upload a photo

    Press 📷 Camera, allow camera access, line yourself up with the dashed guide and tap Capture. Or press 🖼 Upload and pick a photo from your gallery.

    One person only, no hat
  3. 3

    Watch the face scan

    A scan line sweeps your photo while 468 points are mapped across your face. Measurement lines then show your cheeks, jaw, forehead and face length. This takes a few seconds.

  4. 4

    Check the photo check

    Six ticks confirm your head is straight, facing the camera, at the right distance, with shoulders visible, headroom and centred. If anything shows red, tap ↺ Retake photo for better placement.

  5. 5

    Read your face analysis

    You get your face shape (oval, round, square, heart or oblong), jawline width, forehead width and length-to-width ratio. These decide which styles are suggested for you.

  6. 6

    Pick a hairstyle

    Tap one of the Best for you chips, or browse all 381 styles below the photo. Filter by category, search by name, and press Show more to see more. Tapping a style starts the AI.

    Try a few, results are saved per style
  7. 7

    Choose a hair colour

    Pick any of the 16 swatches under Hair colour, from natural browns to rose pink and ice blue. Changing the colour re-generates your current style after a short pause.

  8. 8

    Wait for the AI, then compare

    The AI restyles your real photo in about 15 to 30 seconds while a shimmer plays. When it appears, press and hold the image to flip back to your original and see the difference.

  9. 9

    See every angle and save

    Use the Front · Left · Right tabs above the photo. Each side view takes another 15 to 30 seconds the first time. Press 🔄 Generate all views to make both, then ⬇ Save preview or 🧩 Save 3-view collage.

    Side views are AI estimates of the style

Tips & troubleshooting

How can I test styles instantly?

Tick ⚡ Quick preview in the panel. Styles are drawn on your face right away with no AI, so it is fast but rough. Untick it to get the real photo-realistic result.

The result doesn't look like me

Retake your photo in brighter, even light, facing straight with hair off your face. A new photo gives a fresh result. Glasses and facial hair are kept, and very busy backgrounds can confuse the AI.

I see an error message

The message at the bottom-left of the photo says what went wrong. If it mentions the server or AI being unavailable, wait a minute and tap the style again. You will still get the quick preview in the meantime.

How accurate are the left and right views?

Your photo only shows the front, so the AI estimates the sides using your face and your new hairstyle. Treat them as a preview of the style, not an exact copy of how your hair will fall.

Are my photos stored?

Your photo is sent to an AI service only to create the preview. This site does not save your photos or your results.

Photo protocol

Follow these so the hair lands exactly on your head.

  1. Face the camera straight. Chin level, head not tilted or turned.
  2. Show head and both shoulders. Hold the phone at eye level, arm's length away.
  3. Leave space above your head. Don't crop the top of your hair.
  4. Hair off your forehead and ears. No hats or headbands.
  5. Even light on your face. Face a window or lamp; avoid harsh shadows.
  6. Neutral expression, one person only.
